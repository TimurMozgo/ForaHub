Every case now has five NFTs, four ordinary gifts and Stars. The former per-prize 50% cap is superseded by an expected-cost cap. Contents and outcomes use the same positive integer ticket weights. Probabilities are retained in the draw model but not displayed in the UI. Redo is first.

Current status: paid openings are visual only; no real payments, wallet debits, transferable inventory or withdrawals. Daily visual outcomes and cooldown persist server-side. Current animations do NOT advance paid loyalty progress.

Loyalty: one NFT with actual cost>0 and<=500 per account after2500 net paid Stars spent. Before real sales: verified Telegram settlement; atomic wallet debit plus owned-prize reservation; server-only outcomes; idempotency; stock/inventory/withdrawal liabilities; refunds; funded reserve for the largest possible obligation. When funding is insufficient, pause NEW openings rather than altering advertised odds or replacing winners. Loyalty functions must be integrated atomically with verified settlement and a globally unique owned NFT. After-award refunds require a liability policy without allowing another bonus.

Tests: check-prizes.mjs tests every interval boundary including rarest NFTs, all categories, visible nonzero chances, assets and expected cost+loyalty cap. check-daily.mjs tests concurrent retries/cooldown. check-loyalty.mjs tests threshold, paid-only accounting, refunds, duplicate protection and auth.

ACCOUNT SETTLEMENT UPDATE
New openings are server-authoritative and durably credited to the internal hub account. Atomic CAS on case-account:sites:USER stores debit, Stars reward/inventory quantity, receipt and daily cooldown together. Retry request UUID returns the original receipt even after newer openings; a UUID reused for another case is rejected. The client cannot supply the prize or credit amount.
Paid cases require sufficient internal earned balance and debit their listed price. They no longer offer unlimited free visual rolls. All currently credited funds are reward-funded, not paid Telegram deposits, so these spends do NOT count toward the paid loyalty bonus. No deposits, Telegram transfers or withdrawal are connected; NFT entries are hub reward records, not proof of ownership of an on-chain asset. Historical paid visual results were never stored and are not backfilled. Existing daily cooldown is preserved.
GET /api/cases/account exposes current balance, grouped inventory and latest20 receipts, authenticated/no-store. POST /api/cases/open is same-origin authenticated; /daily is a compatibility wrapper for case0. The embedded scene accepts only the persisted result for its selected case, and cannot start a new paid draw on its own. Amounts survive reload even if animation is interrupted. Local pending UUIDs allow recovery from a lost network response without duplicate debit. The initial internal balance is zero; no free seed credit is fabricated.
A snapshot stores up to5000 receipts and fails closed at capacity; production needs a paginated ledger table before that limit. check-account.mjs covers atomicity/races, all award types, quantity aggregation, retries including older receipts, paid balance checks, auth and account isolation. The real reserve/owned-stock protections above are still required before purchases or transferable issuance.

Daily cooldown is visibly counted down in HH:MM:SS using server clock offset. Paid NFT pools are disjoint except Redo; daily unchanged.
